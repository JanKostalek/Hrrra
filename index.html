    Rules

    Hazards & Bonuses

    Money Bag: +2000 score.

    Coin: +200 score.

    Live: restore 1 lost life up to max.

    Slow: reduces current game speed for a short time.

    Blocker: do not touch, always jump over it.

    Projectile: do not touch.

    Special Mechanics

    Cracked Coin: removes 33% of score earned in this level.

    Question Coin: gamble for a big score win or loss.

    Curse: normal score gain is frozen for a short time.

    Shield: blocks one deadly hit and can save you from bottom death zone.

    Skin Unlock: collect it to unlock a new character skin.
