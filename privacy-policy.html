Hrrra Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile and web versions of Hrrra handle user data.

Overview

Hrrra is a simple arcade platform game. The game itself does not require a user account or direct sign-in.

Information Collected

Hrrra does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address inside the game.

The game may process limited technical data required for gameplay and app operation, such as local game settings and saved scores stored on your device.

Advertising

The Android version of Hrrra uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may collect and process device and advertising data in accordance with Google's own policies in order to serve, limit, or measure ads.

Depending on your region, you may be asked for consent regarding personalized advertising and privacy choices. Privacy options may also be made available again inside the app.

Local Storage

Hrrra stores gameplay-related data locally on the device or in the browser, including items such as:

  • best scores
  • selected game settings
  • admin or tuning preferences used for testing

This data is used only to preserve gameplay state and preferences.

Children

Hrrra is not specifically directed at children under 13. If regional rules require different consent handling, the advertising and privacy systems used by the app may request additional choices before ads are shown.

Third-Party Services

Hrrra may rely on third-party services, including Google Play and Google AdMob. These services may process technical or advertising-related data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect changes in the game, advertising setup, or legal requirements. Any updated version will be published at this page.
